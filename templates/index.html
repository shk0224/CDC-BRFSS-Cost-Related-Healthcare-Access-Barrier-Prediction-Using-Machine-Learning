About this tool. An XGBoost model trained on the CDC Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) 2024 (n = 455,997) and validated on BRFSS 2025. Held-out ROC-AUC 0.84; risks are calibrated. Race and ethnicity are not used as inputs.

This is a population-health screening aid, not medical, financial or eligibility advice. It describes people's reported experience in the past 12 months and does not predict the future. It detects cost barriers less well among insured and higher-income adults.
